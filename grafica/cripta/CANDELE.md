# Cripta: candele nere per la pagina iniziale

Le candele fluttuano sopra il titolo e in fondo alla pagina. La fiamma la disegno io a codice (tremola e fa luce), quindi l'immagine deve avere **solo il corpo della candela con lo stoppino spento**.

Da incollare nella chat di ChatGPT della Cripta, dopo il blocco base del tema (`grafica/PROMPT-TEMI.md`).

```
Game asset for a mobile fantasy companion app, theme "Cripta" (dark necromantic magic). Painterly-realistic dark fantasy style with rich detail; eerie but elegant, not cartoon, not a photo. Soft light from the top-left plus a faint violet rim light on the right edge.

A sprite sheet of 5 separate floating BLACK wax candles, arranged in a single row on a grid, evenly spaced, not touching each other.
- Each candle is a straight cylinder seen from the front, slightly from below, tall and slim (about 1 : 4 width to height), with different heights (short, medium, tall).
- Black wax with a soft satin sheen and a subtle deep violet reflection; slightly melted, uneven top rim; a few thick wax drips running down the sides (black, glossy), one candle with a small drip pooling near the bottom edge.
- A short dark wick at the top, UNLIT: no flame, no glow, no smoke, no light on the top.
- Bottom of the candle cut clean and slightly rounded, no candle holder, no plate, no base: the candles float in the air.
- No cast shadow, no reflection on the ground, no background objects.

Fully transparent background (real PNG alpha, no checkerboard), every candle fully visible with a small empty margin, not cropped. Canvas 2048 x 1024. No text, no logos, no symbols.
```

Se esce con la fiamma o con la luce in cima: «the wicks must be unlit: no flame, no glow». Se le candele si toccano o sono su un piattino: «separate candles, no holder, no plate, floating».
